# Code of Conduct

We want participation in quic-affinity to be safe, constructive, and welcoming.

Be respectful and technically honest. Discuss ideas and observable behavior,
not personal attributes. Welcome questions, give actionable feedback, respect
different experience levels, and assume good intent while still addressing
harmful behavior directly.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable in
project spaces. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other communication made on behalf of the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary. To report conduct concerns,
contact a maintainer privately through the hosting platform. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical, with attention to the safety of the
people involved.
